Prayer times, Hijri dates and Qibla

Prayer times are astronomical calculations based on your location and chosen settings, and may differ by a few minutes from your local mosque or official authority. Hijri dates are calculated using the Umm al-Qura calendar and may differ by a day from local moon sighting. The Qibla direction relies on your device’s compass, which can be affected by magnetic interference. Please verify with your local mosque or official authority, especially during Ramadan and Eid.
